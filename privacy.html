Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Who we are

Shorts Uploader is operated by ethernetrj45 (the "Operator"), based in Spain. Contact: jucalcres@gmail.com.

Data we process

  • When you authorize the app through TikTok Login Kit: your TikTok open_id, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): display name and avatar, shown only to identify the connected account.
  • The video files you choose to upload.

How we use it

Solely to upload videos to the drafts of the connected TikTok account (video.upload). We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we never sell or share it with third parties.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored locally on the Operator's own computer, not on this website, and are deleted when you revoke access or on request. This website does not use cookies, trackers or analytics, and has no database.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification or deletion of your data by emailing jucalcres@gmail.com. You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

Changes

Updates will be published on this page with a new date.

Política de privacidad (ES)
